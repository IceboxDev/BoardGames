# The Hunger — rules reference

A structured transcription of the full 8-page rulebook (Richard Garfield, Origames 2021; English edition
Renegade Game Studios), including what its diagrams and examples show. 2–6 players, 10+, ~60 min.

Table rulings on points the rulebook leaves open live in `rulings.ts`; where the engine differs from this
text, `rulings.ts` says so.

## Introduction and goal

Vampires wake as the eternal roses bloom. Until sunrise you run to the Labyrinth to pick a Rose, hunt
Humans on the way, seduce Familiars, grow your Powers — and get back to the Castle before daybreak.

The Hunger is a race in which each player must:

- optimise their card deck;
- hunt Humans for Victory Points;
- fulfil secret Missions;
- eventually acquire a Rose;
- return to the Castle before sunrise.

The more you hunt, the slower you **and your deck** become, so returning before daybreak gets harder. Every
player starts with a similar deck; choices during the game change its abilities, its Speed, and your
end-game score.

You have **exactly 15 turns** to hunt, score, and get back toward the Castle.

### Modes

- **Rookie Mode** (board side A): to score you must end in the Castle, the Cemetery, **or the Mountains**.
- **Elder Mode** (board side B, the default; the rules below mean Elder unless stated): you must end in the
  Castle or the Cemetery.

## Components

- 122 Hunt cards: 80 Humans, 22 Familiars, 20 Powers
- 6 Vampire sheets, 6 Starting decks of 6 cards, 6 Vampire tokens, 6 Score tokens
- 1 board, 3 Rose cards, 1 Moon token, 50 Mission tiles, 5 Castle tiles, 26 Bonus tokens
- 1 modular Hunt Track, assembled by player count

## Card anatomy

**Starting cards** (golden background, **S** marker): Speed (upper left), name, effect, owner symbol, type
icon (lower right). Example — Vampire Thirst: Speed 1, or 3 if you have at least one Human in your playing
area.

**Hunt cards**: Speed, name, category, type, Victory Points, effect, timing icons.

- VP on a card are gained **as soon as you hunt it** (a Rose: as its rules say).
- A **dark-framed** effect is a Hunting effect.
- Some effects score only at final scoring (End-of-Game).
- Markers: Hunting effect, Playing Area effect, End-of-Game effect, Rookie **A**, Starting **S**.

**Categories / types**: Human — Villager, Religious, Military, Noble; Starting Power; Power; Familiar;
Item. Missions and board spaces refer to these.

## Setup

1. **Board and Hunt Track.** Rookie: side A; Elder: side B. The Hunt Track has **one more row than the
   number of players** (3 players → 4 rows).
2. **Vampires.** Each player takes a sheet, its Starting deck, its token and a Score token on 0.
3. **Hunt deck.** Shuffle all Hunt cards except the Roses, face down.
   - *Rookie:* take all Humans, Familiars and Powers marked **A**, shuffle them, set aside **2 + 2 per
     player** unseen, shuffle the rest of the deck and put the set-aside A cards **on top** (so the first
     draws favour Rookie Powers and Familiars).
4. **Bonus tokens.** Shuffle face down; one on every Chest space; return the rest unseen. Reveal the tokens
   on **open Chests**.
5. **Roses.** All 3 face up on the Labyrinth.
6. **Tavern.** 3 cards face down (Elder: from the top of the deck; Rookie: from the bottom), unseen.
7. **Moon** on Turn 1 — the 15-turn clock.
8. **Castle tiles** by player count: 2p 10, 6 · 3p 10, 6, 4 · 4p 10, 8, 6, 4 · 5–6p 10, 8, 6, 4, 2.
   Returning Vampires take them highest first.
9. **Missions.** With 2–4 players remove the tiles marked **5+**. Take the beige Missions, randomly place
   **2 face up** as Public Missions (Rookie: from those with a white title), then shuffle all the rest.
10. **Personal Mission.** Each player draws 2, keeps 1, returns the other to the box unseen.
11. **Crypts.** Mission stacks: **Mountains 6, Plains 5, Forest 4**. The rest go back unseen.
12. **Starting hand.** Shuffle your Starting deck, draw 3.
13. **First-turn order (Turn 1 only):** total the Speed of your 3 cards; **lowest plays first**. Ties: the
    older player first. Stack the tokens on the Castle, first player on top. (Example: totals 6, 7, 8, 8 →
    the 8s are ordered by age.)
14. **Hunt Track.** One card per row, face up, in **column 3**.

## Mission tiles

- **Standard** (beige): kept hidden until final scoring.
- **Instant** (gold): revealed during your turn; can never be Public; once used, cannot be returned when
  exchanging Missions.

Examples: *Royal* — 1 VP per relevant target hunted. *Strategist* — 6 VP if you hunted more than each other
Vampire. *Vampires of the Coast* (Instant) — after hunting a Human on the Plains, hunt a card in the same
column for free.

## How to play

Players take turns in order; a player resolves everything in their playing area before the next begins.

### A. Turn order

- Turn 1: by starting Speed (setup step 13).
- Later turns, by board position — **the Vampire furthest from the Castle generally plays first**:
  1. region: **Forest, Plains, Mountains, Cemetery**;
  2. same region: path **Road, Railroad, Boat**;
  3. same region and path: the Vampire **closest to the Labyrinth** first;
  4. same space: the token on top first.
- After your turn, flip your token to its Resting side.

(Illustrated example: Koni and Richard are in the Forest, so both go before the Plains Vampires; Koni on a
Road beats Richard on a Railroad. George and Simon are both on the Plains Railroad; George is closer to the
Labyrinth, so George goes first.)

### B. Your turn

At the start of your turn, play **all cards in your hand** into your playing area and resolve them one at a
time in the order you choose. A discard effect may discard a card **before it resolves**, avoiding its
effect. You need not spend all your Speed, but once you trigger a board effect or start hunting you can no
longer move, and unused Speed is lost at the end of the turn. With the Moon on **15**, this is the last
normal turn.

#### Step 1 — discard/draw effects

Resolve discard and draw effects **before anything else**, in any order, but finish one card's effect before
starting another. You may discard only cards whose effect you have **not yet activated**; a card discarded
that way gives neither its effect nor its Speed. If you must draw from an empty deck, shuffle your discard
pile into a new deck and keep drawing.

(Example: Theresa, a Confuse Human, is in your playing area. Vampire Strength draws 1 card because a Human
is present; then Vampiric Will discards Theresa and draws another — Theresa is gone before her Confuse
resolves.)

#### Step 2 — Speed, movement, hunting

Add the Speed of every card in your playing area, **Permanent cards included** (e.g. +2 +2 −1 = 3).
Spend it on moving and/or one Hunt.

**Moving**

- Movement is optional unless a card says otherwise.
- Speed **0 or less**: you cannot move, and the space you stay on does not trigger.
- Positive Speed: move any amount **in one general direction** — you may change paths at an intersection,
  but never go forward and then back along the same path.
- Only the space where you **finish** triggers; spaces passed over do not.

**Pushing.** If you finish on a space with one or more Vampires, you may push them, one at a time from the
top token down, each to any adjacent space (not necessarily the same one). A pushed Vampire does not push
anyone; it is placed on top of whoever is already there. Vampires **in the Cemetery or the Castle cannot
be pushed**.

**Hunting.** After moving, if you still have Speed, you may make **one Hunt**: choose one card or pile on the
Hunt Track and pay its column cost (**1, 2 or 3 Speed**). A **Rose costs 0**. You take **every card** in the
chosen space into your discard pile. After hunting, all remaining Speed is lost and you cannot move.

**Human scoring.** Humans score their printed VP immediately, plus a regional bonus: **Plains +1, Forest
+2** per Human.

(Example: Richard has 2 Speed after moving and takes a 3-card pile from the 1-Speed column containing
Bernard 3 VP and Ivo 2 VP, in the Forest: 3 + 2 + 4 = 9 VP. He loses his last Speed and his turn ends.)

#### Board effects

- **Castle:** you cannot hunt there (the engine rules the Castle a Well — see `rulings.ts`). Arriving, take
  the **highest Castle tile left** and score it immediately. You can never leave the Castle, and cannot be
  pushed out. The Castle contains a **Well** (for Spicy and Mist).
- **Cemetery:** you cannot hunt Humans there; you cannot be pushed there.
- **Chest:** if a Bonus token remains, take it face up and score **2 VP** at once. No limit on tokens held.
- **Crypt:** Missions (procedure below).
- **Labyrinth:** hunt **one Rose of your choice for free**; it is your Hunt for the turn, so no Hunt Track
  hunt that turn.
- **Market / Church / Mansion / Barracks:** each matches one Human category; landing there lets you
  **Digest one Human of that category** from your discard pile or playing area — even one hunted this turn.
- **Ship:** you cannot hunt there.
- **Tavern:** instead of hunting the Track, spend **2 Speed** to hunt **all cards in the Tavern** (face down
  until someone takes them; you must take them all).
- **Well:** an **extra Hunt** this turn, from **column 1** only.

#### Missions at a Crypt

Pick up **all** Mission tiles on that space, add them to your hand, keep what you want and put the rest
back: you may swap out one or more — even all — of your unused Missions, taking the same number of new
ones. You may not reveal the ones you put back. (Example: the Mountains Crypt has 6; you hold 1; you look at
7, return 5 and keep 2 — your old one plus a new one, or two new ones.)

*Rookie:* look at the available Missions, take exactly 1, return the others; you may only return Missions you
just picked up.

### Card types

- **Humans** — the main targets: VP, good or bad effects, Mission progress. Hunt selectively for score and
  Mission synergy.
- **Powers** — bat form, blending into darkness, more Speed, strength, deck efficiency. Useful, but don't
  neglect scoring.
- **Familiars** — helpers, generally **Permanent**: once played they stay in your playing area.
- **Roses** — from the Labyrinth; useful permanent bonuses. They count as **Items**. Optional, but described
  as a significant advantage. Taking one costs no Speed but **counts as your Hunt**.

#### Step 3 — end of turn

1. Flip your token to Resting.
2. Discard your playing area **except Permanent cards**.
3. Resolve end-of-turn effects (e.g. Eternal Rose: 1 VP).
4. Draw 3 cards, reshuffling your discard pile if the deck runs out.

### C. Preparing the next turn

- **Advance the Moon.** If it was on 15, the game ends — except that a Vampire with a **Parasol** may take
  one more turn, **without hunting**.
- **Reactivate** every Vampire.
- **Shift the Hunt Track** one column right. Column-1 cards stay and **pile up**. Players may always inspect a
  pile before hunting it.
- **Refill** column 3 with one card per row — **except after Turn 15**.
- **Refill the Tavern** with 1 face-down card if no Vampire is in the Tavern and it holds fewer than 3.

## Sunrise and scoring

Where you finish:

- **Plains or Forest:** burned to ashes. Your points still count "for the glory of it", but you are not a
  survivor (the engine ranks the burnt below every survivor — see `scoring.ts`).
- **Mountains:** Rookie — you lose the VP printed on your space; Elder — burned to ashes. A group may rule
  that beginners in Elder Mode are safe in the Mountains (`beginnerSafeMountains`).
- **Cemetery:** safe; **−5 VP**.
- **Castle:** safe.

### Final scoring

- **Card bonuses:** End-of-Game effects. Cyrana: 4 VP if you also have Roxane. Hunters score by how many
  Humans of their type you have (the Hunter counts itself). Echo scores per Wolf you have (itself included).
- **Public Missions:** everyone checks both.
- **Personal Missions:** reveal and score those completed.
- **Majority Missions** need **strictly more** than every other Vampire.
- Scores over 99: flip the Score token to its 100 side.

**Winner:** the most VP. Ties: the one who reached the Castle first; if neither did, whoever is closest to
the Castle.

### Full turn example (Koni)

No discard/draw effects. With a Human in play, Vampire Thirst gives 3 Speed; The Hunger gives 2: 5 Speed.
She moves 3 spaces onto a Crypt and takes a Mission, then spends 1 of her 2 remaining Speed on a
column-1 pile holding two Humans worth 3 + 3 = 6 VP, +2 for the Plains, +2 from The Hunger: 10 VP.

## Bonus tokens

Collecting one scores **2 VP** unless stated otherwise. It can later be used once during your turn (flip it
to its Chest side).

- **Human type** (Religious, Military, Villager, Noble): counts as one Human of that type for Missions and
  majorities; not a card in your deck.
- **Human Type Choice:** one extra Human of a type you choose **at the end of the game**; may serve several
  Missions; fixed once chosen.
- **+1 / +2 Speed:** that much Speed this turn.
- **+1 Hunt:** one extra Hunt this turn.
- **Discard / Draw:** discard 1 card, draw 1.
- **Draw:** draw 1 card straight into your playing area.
- **Gain 1 Mission:** take a Mission from any stack as if you stood on that Crypt.
- **Parasol:** one extra turn after the normal last turn, without hunting. Several Parasols still give one.
- **Velvet Clothing:** +2 VP more on collection (4 VP total).

## Effect icons and keywords

- **Discard 1 card:** move another card from your playing area to your discard pile — possibly before it
  resolves, to avoid its effect. Permanent cards may be discarded. Some effects discard themselves to
  trigger.
- **Familiar:** Permanent.
- **Rose:** an Item; costs no Speed; counts as a Hunt.
- **End-of-Game:** resolved at the end; a Human with one still earned its regional bonus when hunted.
- **Effect on the Hunt Track:** acts while the card is on the Track or when it is hunted, per its text.
- **CONFUSE:** before calculating Speed, move **4 spaces toward the Labyrinth**; the space you land on does
  not trigger and you push no one; then play on normally. Not cumulative (two Confuse cards still move 4);
  never beyond the Labyrinth.
- **DIGEST:** move a Human from your playing area or discard pile into your **Digestion zone**. It still
  counts for Missions and End-of-Game effects but never returns to your deck — thinning out slow Humans
  while keeping their score.
- **FAST:** a pile with one or more Fast cards costs **+1 Speed** (once).
- **GREGARIOUS:** when hunted, draw the top Hunt card into your discard pile; if it is a Human, score it
  normally (regional bonus included) and resolve its effects.
- **HOLY WATER:** with one in your playing area you **cannot hunt** that turn.
- **INSPIRING:** take a Mission from any stack as if you stood on that Crypt.
- **PERMANENT:** never discarded at end of turn; stays in play (effect and Speed every turn) until
  something removes it.
- **READY:** when gained, put it on top of your deck or in your discard pile.
- **SLOW:** enters the Hunt Track directly in **column 2** (possibly forming a pile).
- **SPICY:** you must spend Speed moving toward the **closest Well**, even behind you or on another path. If
  you can't reach it, spend all your Speed toward it and keep carrying the card; on reaching a Well you
  stop there and may hunt with any Speed left. Equidistant Wells: your choice. Already on a Well: no move,
  but you may hunt.
- **UNIQUE:** you cannot own two Unique cards of the same type and category — currently only Roses.

## Named examples from the rulebook

- **Vampire Thirst** (Starting): Speed 1, or 3 with a Human in your playing area.
- **Vampire Strength** (Starting): Speed 2; draw 1 if a Human is in your playing area.
- **The Hunger** (Starting): Speed 2; +1 VP per Human you hunt this turn.
- **Vampiric Will:** discard 1, draw 1.
- **Form of Mist:** instead of moving, teleport to the next Well in either direction.
- **Form of Bat:** while moving, skip spaces holding Wells or Vampires.
- **Vampiric Stealth:** an extra Hunt this turn.
- **Eternal Rose** (Ready, Unique, Permanent): 1 VP at the end of each of your turns.
- **Dead Rose** (Ready, Unique, Permanent): 1 VP at the end of turns in which you hunt.
- **Perfect Rose** (Ready, Unique, Permanent): 2 VP at the end of turns in which you don't hunt.
- **Kutya** (Ready, Permanent): an extra column-1 Hunt.
- **Bernard, Eunice:** Spicy. **Calvin:** Fast. **Theresa:** Confuse. **Bolat:** Holy Water. **Patricia:**
  Slow.
- **Echo:** Permanent Wolf; 1 VP at the end per Wolf you have.
- **Cyrana:** 4 VP at the end if you have Roxane. **Baron Christien:** a similar Roxane condition.

## The central tension

Draw 3 → discard/draw manipulation → total Speed → travel out or in → trigger the space you stop on →
optionally hunt → Humans enter your deck and make it slower → end turn → the Track shifts, the Moon
advances → race back to safety before Turn 15. **Hunting more raises your score but fills your deck with
slow Humans, making the trip home harder.**
