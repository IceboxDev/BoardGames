> (card: [Engineering and Inventions] If a commercial airplane suddenly runs out of fuel during a flight, what will most likely happen?
A. It will lose directional thrust and follow an erratic flight path
B. It will glide
C. It will slow down and then plummet
Answer: B. Modern airplanes are designed to glide without engine power. Pilots are trained to handle such emergencies by finding the best possible landing site and safely gliding the aircraft to the ground. Most commercial aircraft today have a glide ratio of around 15:1, meaning they can travel 15 miles horizontally for every mile they descend.)
> notes: The Gimli Glider glided from 41,000 ft to a safe landing. The card's 15:1 glide ratio is a rough typical figure, not checked against a source.
