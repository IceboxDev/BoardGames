> Welche japanische Gedichtform ist Matsuo Bashōs „Ein alter stiller Teich / Ein Frosch springt in den Teich / Platsch! Wieder Stille“? → **Ein Haiku**
> What form of Japanese poem is Matsuo Bashō's "An old silent pond / A frog jumps into the pond / Splash! Silence again"? → **A haiku**
> (card: [Literature] What type of literary composition is the following? “An old silent pond/ A frog jumps into the pond/ Splash! Silence again.”
A. A haiku B. A ballad C. An ode
Answer: A. Haiku is a form of poetry that originated in Japan, and its traditional subjects include nature, the seasons, and the fleeting nature of existence. A haiku traditionally consists of three lines with a 5-7-5 syllable structure. This Haiku was composed by Matsuo Bashō in the 1600s.)
> notes: In Bashō's day it was a hokku; the name haiku came from Masaoka Shiki in the late 19th century. The 5-7-5 count is of Japanese morae (on), not English syllables — the card's translation doesn't fit it.
