> (card: [Agronomy] Which ancient civilization is known for creating floating gardens, known as chinampas, for agriculture?
A. Aztec B. Maya C. Inca
Answer: A. Chinampas were constructed by layering mud and vegetation on shallow lake beds, creating fertile plots that allowed the Aztecs to grow crops like maize, tomatoes, chili peppers, and even flowers. They significantly increased agricultural yields and supported the large population of the Aztec capital, Tenochtitlan.)
> notes: Card is right on the civilization, but the Aztecs did not invent chinampas — they were the first to use them for large-scale cultivation (Wikipedia); 'ancient' is loose for the Aztec Empire (1428–1521).
