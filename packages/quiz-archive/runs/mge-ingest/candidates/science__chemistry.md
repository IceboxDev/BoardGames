## { "card": "ppm-141", "category": 1 } · Upper question

> Was geschieht mit dem pH-Wert von reinem Wasser, wenn es über 25 °C erwärmt wird, obwohl das Wasser neutral bleibt? → **Er sinkt unter 7**
> What happens to the pH of pure water when it is heated above 25 °C, even though the water stays neutral? → **It falls below 7**
> (card: [Chemistry] How does the pH of pure water change as the temperature increases?
A. It remains constant
B. It decreases
C. It increases
Answer: B. As the temperature of pure water increases, the ionization of water molecules increases, leading to more hydrogen ions (H⁺) and hydroxide ions (OH⁻). As pH ("power of Hydrogen”) measures the hydrogen ion concentration, it decreases. However, the water does not become more acidic, because the relative concentrations of H⁺ and OH⁻ remain equal. In this case, the interpretation of the pH scale itself shifts.)
