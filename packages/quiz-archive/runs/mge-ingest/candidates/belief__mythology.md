> What kind of traditional story — like a tale of a sun goddess whose tears of fire created the first volcanoes — explains natural events or origins and usually features gods or supernatural beings? → **A myth**
> (card: [Literature] What type of narrative is the following? “Long ago, the sun goddess Solaria wept tears of fire that fell to the earth, creating the first volcanoes. Whenever Solaria mourns the loss of a hero, a volcano erupts.”
A. A myth
B. A legend
C. A fable
Answer: A. A myth is a story that explains natural events, cultural practices, or the origins of the world. It often involves gods or supernatural beings. A legend is an embellished story based on historical events, and a fable usually highlights a moral message.)
> notes: Card confirmed: myths' main characters are usually gods and other supernatural figures, origin myths explain how things came to be; legends are mostly secular with human protagonists (Bascom 1965). The card's Solaria story is invented.
