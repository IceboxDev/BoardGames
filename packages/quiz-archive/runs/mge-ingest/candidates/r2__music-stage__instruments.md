> (card: [Musicology] What is a tritone interval often referred to as in music?
A. Perfect fifth
B. Minor third
C. Devil’s interval
Answer: C. Due to its dissonant sound, the tritone interval is famously used in the opening theme of “The Simpsons” (between the first two melody notes: “The Simp-”) to introduce the audience to its unsettling and chaotic world.)
