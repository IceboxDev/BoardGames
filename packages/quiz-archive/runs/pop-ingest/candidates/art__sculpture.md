> (card: [Engineering and Invention] How was the Statue of Liberty constructed?
A. Solid bronze cast in multiple sections
B. Reinforced concrete with a steel skeleton
C. Iron frame with a thin copper layer
Answer: C. The Statue of Liberty was constructed with an iron framework designed by Gustave Eiffel, the same engineer behind the Eiffel Tower. A thin layer of copper, only 2.4 millimeters thick, was then hammered into place to form the statue’s outer surface. This innovation allowed the statue to withstand various weather conditions and the passage of time.)
