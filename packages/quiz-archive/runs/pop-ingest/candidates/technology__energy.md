> (card: [Engineering and Invention] What are modern solar panels usually made from?
A. Graphite
B. Silicon
C. Nickel
Answer: B. Silicon is a semiconductor material that can efficiently convert sunlight into electricity. Silicon solar cells work by absorbing photons from sunlight, which then knock electrons loose from the silicon atoms. > notes: Crystalline silicon has about a 95% market share of commercial PV cells.
