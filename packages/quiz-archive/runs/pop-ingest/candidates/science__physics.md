> According to the Boltzmann distribution, how are atoms distributed over the available energy states as the temperature approaches infinity? → **Evenly across all states**
> (card: [Physics] If you heat a gas to infinitely high temperatures, what is the distribution of the atoms' energy states?
A. Most atoms are in low-energy states
B. Most atoms are in high-energy states
C. Atoms are evenly distributed across all energy states
Answer: C. According to the Boltzmann distribution, the energy levels of the atoms in a gas are equally populated at infinitely high temperatures. This means that each energy state has the same number of atoms, leading to a state of maximum entropy or disorder.)
> notes: Confirmed for systems with a limited number of energy states: as T rises, the populations of lower and higher states approach equality (P ∝ e^(−E/kT) → 1).
