## How the cards were produced

One background agent per photo. Each agent rotated the photo 90° left with ImageMagick, read the full card plus three overlapping band crops with vision (no OCR engine is installed), transcribed the 12 German questions verbatim, mapped them to the 12 categories by position (pink/blue/sand band × TL/TR/BL/BR quadrant), translated them, wrote four sibling questions per original in the same form and sub-topic, answered all five in German and English, wrote a ~400–600-word English article containing all five answers verbatim and translated it into German containing all five German answers verbatim, then ran the self-check until it passed. A handful of cards were rerun on Sonnet after Opus's output filter rejected them; one batch was relaunched after an overnight usage-limit stop, and 13 workers of the second run were cut off by a session-limit reset on 2026-09-17 and relaunched the same evening (they reused their rotated images and any verified fragments; two of them had already written a passing JSON, which the coordinator reviewed and finished directly). Nothing was committed or pushed.
