  - stored card text: [Chemistry] How does the pH of pure water change as the temperature increases? / A. It remains constant / B. It decreases / C. It increases / Answer: B. As the temperature of pure water increases, the ionization of water molecules increases, leading to more hydrogen ions (H⁺) and hydroxide ions (OH⁻). As pH ("power of Hydrogen”) measures the hydrogen ion concentration, it decreases. However, the water does not become more acidic, because the relative concentrations of H⁺ and OH⁻ remain equal. In this case, the interpretation of the pH scale itself shifts.
