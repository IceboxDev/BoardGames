## The question
On 22 October 1688 James II summoned an extraordinary meeting of the Privy Council (with peers and others present) at Whitehall, where people who had attended Queen Mary of Modena at the birth of James Francis Edward Stuart (10 June 1688) gave sworn depositions, to rebut the "warming-pan" rumour that the baby had been smuggled in. Our quiz asks: **How many witnesses testified at that meeting that they had seen the birth?** Our current answer is **42**.

## The dilemma
