## The question
Printed card (Trivial Pursuit, German): "Der Trabant wurde in der DDR genauso oft gebaut wie der VW Käfer in der Bundesrepublik. Richtig oder falsch?" (The Trabant was built in the GDR as often as the VW Beetle in West Germany — true or false?). The statement is false. Our open question asks: **How many cars of the Trabant series were built in Zwickau in total (from 1957 until production ended in 1991)?** Our current answer is **about 3.1 million (3,096,099)**.

## The dilemma
- The figure 3,096,099 comes from adding up model-by-model production figures (German Wikipedia "Trabant (Pkw)" and related tables: P50/P60, 601 variants, 1.1).
- MDR (Mitteldeutscher Rundfunk) speaks of "about three million", in one place "about 3.6 million with predecessor models".
- Deutsche Welle (2007) and parts of English Wikipedia cite 3.7 million.
- It is unclear whether the higher figures include the predecessor AWZ P70 (built in Zwickau 1955–1959, not a Trabant), the Trabant 1.1 (1990–91, VW engine), export/CKD kits, or simply a rounding/copying error.

## What we need settled
- The authoritative total production of Trabant cars (all models P50, P60, 601, 1.1) from primary sources: Sachsenring company records, the August Horch Museum Zwickau, the Bundesarchiv/IFA records, scholarly histories of the GDR car industry, or the BMW/VW/IFA-era publications.
- A per-model breakdown if available, and what exactly each competing total (≈3.0, 3.1, 3.6, 3.7 million) includes or excludes.
- The VW Beetle comparison: how many Beetles were built in West Germany (≈15.8 million) vs worldwide (≈21.5 million) — confirm with a VW source, since the article states both.
